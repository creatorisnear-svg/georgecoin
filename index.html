Chapter oneSunday

Every week, George waits for one day. Not his birthday, not a holiday. Sunday. Sunday is pancake day, and the griddle is hot before the sun is even up.

He wears the hat. He holds the fork like a trophy. He watches the batter bubble like it owes him money.
